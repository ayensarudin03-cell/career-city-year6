Useful sentence patterns

👩‍⚕️ A doctor helps sick people.

👨‍🍳 A chef works in a kitchen.

👩‍🚀 An astronaut needs to be brave and curious.

Grammar tip: a / an

Use an before a vowel sound: an astronaut, an engineer. Use a before a consonant sound: a pilot, a teacher.
